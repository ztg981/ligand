## Latest round (30 Jul, extension)

**The error you saw is from the old copy.** `chrome.alarms` undefined at
`background.js:186` is the missing-permission crash that was fixed in v0.3.0 —
you're still running the build from before the reload. It's now also *guarded*,
because that line sat at the top level of the service worker: throwing there
killed the worker before its other listeners registered, so a single missing
permission took the whole extension down rather than just the badge countdown.
That is what "barely works" was.

**The "Working on" picker no longer fights you.** Picking a task wrote to the
page, then immediately re-read the worker's *cached* snapshot — which hadn't
been updated yet — so the picker snapped back to the previous owner, and the
three-second poll flipped it forward again a moment later. Now the choice is
held on screen until the data agrees, the page pushes a fresh snapshot the
instant a link lands, and the options list is only rebuilt when it actually
changes (rebuilding it mid-interaction was closing the open dropdown).

**You can make the thing you're working on.** "+ New" in the popup creates a
task — optionally under an existing goal, or a brand-new one — with the tab
group already attached, seeded with the page title. One action, not
create-then-link, so the group never points at nothing in between.

Extension is now **v0.4.0**: `git pull`, then ↻ on the Ligand card.

## Previous round (30 Jul, late)

**Focus stats have somewhere to go.** The "Focus" button on the focus widget —
and on the Focus card in Stats — used to jump to the Pomodoro tab, which is the
one place the widget's own numbers didn't lead. It now opens **Focus in
detail**: your streak, personal bests, the hours of the day you actually focus
in, what the time went toward, and your recent sessions. Starting a session is
a button inside it, so the timer is still one click away.

It leads with the streak and the records rather than a weekly total, because
those are the numbers that only go up. A weekly total is as likely to fall as
to rise and reads as a scolding on a quiet week; a personal best is only ever
an achievement. Nothing in there shows a target you missed.

**The data behind it is new, so it fills in from here.** The focus log stored
`{ date, minutes, goalId }` and nothing else — enough for a bar chart and
nothing more. Sessions now also record when they ended, whether the block ran
to the end or you stopped it early (or logged it after the fact), and what you
were focusing on in words. Older entries simply lack those fields and the view
says so rather than drawing a shape from a fraction of your history — the
hour-of-day chart tells you how many sessions it could place.

## Previous round (30 Jul, evening)

**Two new scenes: CA Highway and New York.** Both pure CSS, both with a day and
a night version — the highway runs from a blue summer afternoon to golden hour,
New York from an overcast morning to dusk. The highway aims everything at one
vanishing point (markings, palms, poles and the traffic ahead all run the same
path out of the horizon), which is what makes it read as travelling rather than
as parts sliding about. New York is dusk on purpose: the warm band low in the
sky is the city, and it gives the towers something to be silhouetted against.

**Clip thumbnails show the actual clip now.** They were black, for three
reasons stacked on top of each other: `preload="metadata"` fetches no picture
at all, the `#t=0.1` fragment is ignored on `blob:` URLs, and frame zero of a
recording is black anyway because the camera is still metering. A real frame is
now decoded and drawn to a canvas — and *measured*, so if the frame comes back
blank the clip is played briefly and a later one taken instead.

## Previous round (30 Jul, later)

**The Pomodoro follows you between devices.** Start a block on the laptop,
pause it, open the iPad, resume — it picks up where you left off. The session
record already held an ABSOLUTE end time, so both devices count down to the
same instant and there is nothing to reconcile while it runs; it was simply
excluded from sync on the reasoning that a running timer shouldn't teleport.
It now carries a `savedAt` stamp too, so whoever pressed a button most recently
wins and a device that has been asleep can't yank the timer backwards.

**Sub-minute focus counts.** Anything over a second is logged, and totals under
a minute now read as seconds ("42s") instead of rounding to "1m" — or, under
half a minute, to "0m", which reported real time as none at all.

**Resizing the focus scene is rebuilt.** The old version grabbed the whole
panel, grew both sides at once, and cancelled itself on the slight vertical
wobble at the start of any unhurried drag — hence "you can't even hold and drag
it". Now there's a grab strip down each edge: pull the right one right and the
scene grows rightward, pull the left one left and it grows leftward, with the
opposite edge pinned. Press and hold makes it jiggle, the drag tracks your
pointer exactly, and pushing past the page edge squashes rather than going
dead. The "drag to resize" hint is gone — it's unlabelled now, found by the
resize cursor.

**The dial X is on the time card.** It was on the ring, which was still easy to
miss. The floating "10:00 AM – 12:00 PM · 2h" card now stays up for as long as
a block is selected and carries the X in its corner — that card is the only
thing on the dial that says in words which block you mean.

## Latest round (30 Jul)

**Recording on the deployed site was blocked by a header, not by the code.**
`vercel.json` sent `Permissions-Policy: microphone=()` — an empty allowlist,
which disallows the microphone for the site *itself*. Chrome therefore refused
`getUserMedia` outright and never showed a permission prompt, which is exactly
the "says it needs permission but doesn't ask" report. Now `microphone=(self)`.
This one only ever failed on the deployed site; localhost was unaffected, which
is why it survived so long.

**Focus scene** — widening now widens only. A 16/10 aspect ratio turns extra
width into extra height, so expanding it was also making it ~80% taller and
pushing the transport controls down the page.

**Focus scene, the white corners — this genuinely was not fixed last time.**
The cause is `--shadow-pop`, which opens with an inset near-white highlight
line. That is right for a pale card and wrong for an always-dark scene, where
it wraps the rounded corners. The scene now carries a plain drop shadow. (Last
round's note claiming this was fixed was wrong.)

**Focus scene, resizing** — the expand button is gone. Press the scene and it
jiggles like a home-screen widget; drag sideways and the width follows your
pointer; push past either end and it squashes instead of going dead, then
springs back. Vertical swipes still scroll, and the gesture stays out of the
way where there's no room to grow (phones, hyperfocus). Arrow keys do the same
job from the keyboard.

**Day dial, midnight — also not really fixed last time.** Stopping the
selection from flipping was done by walling the drag off at 12am, which made
11pm–1am impossible to draw at all. Blocks can now genuinely cross midnight, in
either drag direction, and an existing block wraps around the ring instead of
piling up against 12am. A late block's end is stored past 1440 ("into
tomorrow"), and the editor labels it rather than letting `23:00 → 01:00` look
like a typo. One piece was still silently broken underneath: `createDayBlock`
clamped the end to 24h, truncating every late-night block on the way into the
store.

**Day dial, the X** — the remove control was a trash icon in the corner tool
stack: a third identical grey circle next to two dial *preferences*, nowhere
near the thing it removes. It's now a red X pinned to the selected block on the
ring, which follows the block while it's dragged.

**Accent** — the deep purple read as dusty rather than electric. Neon is a
chroma effect, not a lightness one, so the same slot now renders at high chroma
shifted toward magenta. Anyone who already picked it is upgraded in place, and
it's the new-user dark default.

**Site logo** — there was no `/favicon.ico` and no social card, so the deployed
URL was unbranded anywhere that doesn't parse `<link>` tags (crawlers, link
unfurlers in Slack/iMessage/Discord/X). Both are now generated by the same
script as every other icon.

## 1. Chrome extension

**Install:** `chrome://extensions` → Developer mode → **Load unpacked** →
select `extension/`. Pin it. **No publishing needed.**

Open Ligand in a tab; the popup header should say **connected**.

- **Quick task / note / log** — one line, Enter.
- **Tab group → task.** "Working on" assigns the group you're in to a task.
  A task captured inside a group auto-links to it.
- **Pomodoro** — live countdown, **start/pause/skip**, minutes-left badge.
- **Icon wears your accent** — change Ligand's theme, the toolbar icon follows.

Captures made while Ligand is closed queue and flush when a tab opens.

*No backend:* the extension never writes storage itself; the page performs
every write through the real store actions. Works in guest mode, no sign-in.

⚠️ If your live Ligand is on a custom domain (not `*.vercel.app`), add it to
`host_permissions` **and** `content_scripts.matches` in `manifest.json`.

## 2. Pomodoro — a real bug, worth testing

**Start a block, switch to Journal, wait for it to end.**

It used to lose the session entirely: the tab is lazily mounted, so leaving it
tore down the clock *and* the phase-end handler. Nothing chimed and **the focus
time was never logged**. Now it rings and logs wherever you are.

Also: **End session** banks the minutes already spent (unlike Reset, which
discards them), with a receipt. Focus tracking was verified end-to-end from
both routes — ending early, and letting a block finish.

## 3. Journal recordings

Composer → **Record** opens a camera-style sheet: Voice/Video, live preview,
big shutter, timer.

- **Hold to record; slide right to lock** (15s → 3 min when locked).
- **Back camera and sound are the defaults.** An early version recorded video
  silently — a clip of someone talking was worthless. Mute is now an explicit
  toggle for when you want music to keep playing.
- Blobs live in IndexedDB, not the entry (Safari's ~5 MB localStorage cap makes
  base64 media impossible); syncs to Supabase Storage once the migration is in.

## 4. Journal map

An interactive map of where entries were written, with bubbles that re-cluster
as you zoom, plus entries placed / distinct places / where you write most.

Uses Leaflet + OpenStreetMap — Apple and Google both need an external SDK that
this app's `script-src 'self'` policy blocks outright. Lazy-loaded, so you only
pay the 150 KB if you open it.

**Entries written before this have no coordinates and won't appear.** Make a new
entry with location to see it work.

## Known limitations

- Recordings aren't in the JSON export (both Settings screens say so).
- **iOS needs real-device testing**: silent-switch behaviour, recording in a
  standalone PWA, and whether the hold/slide gesture feels right.
- Safari re-asks for camera permission per page load. **Add Ligand to your Home
  Screen** — installed PWAs persist the grant.
- Music resuming after a recording is iOS's call; we release the audio session
  promptly, which is the most the web allows.
- macOS ignores `setIcon` for the Dock, so there only the tray follows the theme.
